Ayurvedic Ingredients

Ayurvedic ingredients are a wide range of plant-based, mineral, and animal products, with plant-based substances making up the majority, including herbs, roots, leaves, fruits, and seeds...

Common Ayurvedic Ingredients:

  • Ashwagandha: Helps manage stress, boosts immunity, improves sleep.
  • Turmeric: Anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, supports liver function.
  • Amla (Indian Gooseberry): Rich in Vitamin C, cooling properties.
  • Brahmi: Enhances memory and cognitive function.
  • Cinnamon: Anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial.
  • Giloy (Guduchi): Boosts immunity, supports digestion.
  • Licorice Root (Mulethi): Cooling, anti-inflammatory, helps infections.
  • Shatavari: Rejuvenating herb for women's health.
  • Honey: Nutritional and medicinal properties.
  • Coriander (Dhanyaka): Cooling effect, aids digestion.
  • Aloe Vera: Healing gel, supports skin and digestive health.

Important Considerations:

  • Regulation: Ayurvedic products are regulated as dietary supplements in the U.S.
  • Safety: Some may contain heavy metals like lead, arsenic, or mercury.
  • Interactions: Can interact with conventional medicines or supplements.
  • Consult a Professional: Always consult a healthcare provider before using Ayurvedic treatments.
